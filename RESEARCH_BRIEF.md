## 6. RAG-Verify — starting design (refine on dev only, before the freeze)

Each module must be switchable for ablation.

- **M1 Uncertainty-gated retrieval.** Closed-book draft + a confidence estimate (token log-probabilities and/or agreement across a few samples). Retrieve only below a dev-tuned threshold.
- **M2 Hybrid retrieval and reranking.** BM25 + dense retrieval fused (e.g., reciprocal rank fusion), cross-encoder reranking to top-k, filtering of low-relevance passages.
- **M3 Evidence-grounded drafting.** Answer only from the evidence, with passage citations and an explicit "insufficient evidence" option.
- **M4 Claim-level external verification.** Split the draft into atomic claims (rules for short answers, an LLM prompt for long ones) and score each claim against its evidence with a small local fact-checking/NLI model. Candidates to verify (availability, licence, size): MiniCheck, AlignScore, HHEM-2.1-Open, DeBERTa-v3 NLI models.
- **M5 Targeted re-retrieval and minimal revision.** For unsupported claims only: focused queries → new evidence → revise only the unsupported spans → re-verify (≤ 2 rounds).
- **M6 Risk-controlled abstention.** Abstain when the aggregated support score is below a threshold calibrated on held-out data for a target hallucination rate α ∈ {5%, 10%, 20%}: conformal risk control for the overall hallucination rate (a monotone loss); Learn-then-Test if controlling the error rate among answered questions. State the exchangeability assumption and report realised risk.

Hypotheses to test, not assume:
- **H1** lower hallucination rate than the best baseline at matched coverage;
- **H2** a small external verifier beats LLM self-verification (CoVe-style) at lower cost;
- **H3** gating cuts retrieval and LLM calls with ≤ 1 point accuracy loss;
- **H4** realised risk on held-out data stays close to α.

**Circularity guard:** the model used inside M4 must not be the evaluator that scores faithfulness — use a different model family for evaluation (§9).

You may change this design after the literature review or dev results if a variant is better, provided it stays retrieval-augmented + externally verified + risk-controlled, every change happens before the freeze, and each change is logged with its reason.

## 7. Baselines (same backbone and retrieval access unless noted)

Cover every family; aim for 7–9 baselines; justify the final list in the protocol before any test run. Methods originally built on closed APIs are re-implemented on our backbone — document every adaptation.

- **No retrieval:** closed-book direct answer.
- **Standard RAG:** top-k; top-k + the same reranker RAG-Verify uses (so gains can't be explained by reranking alone); top-k with a larger k (so gains can't be explained by seeing more passages).
- **Robust/corrective RAG:** CRAG (its released retrieval evaluator if available; its web search replaced by our local index); Chain-of-Note (prompting adaptation); optionally one strong 2025–2026 method from Phase 1.
- **Active/adaptive retrieval:** FLARE; optionally DRAGIN or Adaptive-RAG if feasible.
- **Self-reflective:** Self-RAG with its released 7B checkpoint (it has its own backbone — label it that way).
- **Verification/revision:** Chain-of-Verification (factored variant); RARR or Verify-and-Edit.
- **Abstention baselines:** standard RAG + a token-probability threshold, and + SelfCheckGPT-style sampling consistency, so abstention comparisons are fair.

Every method that issues new retrieval queries (FLARE, CRAG, RARR, RAG-Verify) uses the same local index; static methods use the same pre-retrieved passages.

## 9. Metrics

- **Short-form QA:** accuracy with prior work's exact matching rule (read their eval code); EM and F1; coverage; selective accuracy; hallucination rate = answered-and-wrong ÷ all questions; abstention rate; risk–coverage curves and AURC; matched-coverage comparison against the abstention baselines.
- **Faithfulness:** unsupported-claim rate from an evaluator family different from M4 (preferably two independent checkers); citation precision/recall where applicable.
- **Verification:** label accuracy and macro-F1; detection F1/AUROC on RAGTruth/LLM-AggreFact.
- **Efficiency:** LLM calls, prompt + completion tokens, wall time and GPU-seconds per example, peak VRAM.
- **Statistics:** paired bootstrap 95% CIs (10,000 resamples) on differences; McNemar or paired permutation tests; Holm–Bonferroni across comparisons; mean ± std over 3 seeds for sampled components.
- **LLM-as-judge:** not a primary metric. If used at all, the judge differs from the backbone and the verifier and is validated against ≥ 100 human-labelled items with agreement reported. Prepare `analysis/human_eval_sheet.csv` for me to label.

## 10. Ablations and analyses

- Remove each of M1–M6 in turn; replace the external verifier with LLM self-verification; swap verifier models; vary top-k, revision rounds and thresholds; vary backbone family/size.
- Quantization check: 4-bit vs. the highest precision that fits (8-bit or FP16) on the small backbone (~200 examples).
- Robustness: 0/25/50/75% distractor passages; counterfactual passages; no-evidence condition.
- Efficiency: hallucination and accuracy vs. cost (Pareto plot).
- Calibration: reliability diagram for support scores; realised risk vs. target α.
- Error analysis: stratified sample of 100 failures coded with a written codebook (labelled as model-assisted annotation); qualitative examples copied verbatim from real outputs.
